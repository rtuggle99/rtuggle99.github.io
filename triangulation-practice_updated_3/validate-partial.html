Validate the Partial Triangulation

Practice recognizing valid and invalid partial triangulations of a 2D point set.

A partial triangulation of a 2D point set P is a nonempty set of triangles such that

  • each triangle's vertices are points of P, with no other point of P inside the triangle,
  • its triangles meet only at shared edges or vertices, and
  • its boundary edges (those belonging to exactly one triangle) form a single loop in which each boundary vertex belongs to exactly two boundary edges.
