Order the Children

Practice finding the children of a node in order.

The partial triangulation below has its candidate-only (dashed) and real (solid) children displayed underneath it. They are arranged in a random order; rearrange them in the correct order. "Lock in" checks the leftmost unconfirmed child. A correct guess locks it in place; an incorrect guess has you try again. If you get stuck, hit "Show answer" to reveal the next child.

Ear pass first: from the head, walk the boundary counterclockwise edge by edge, trying every unused point as the third vertex (in left-to-right order, breaking ties by which point is lower) before moving to the next edge.

Then link pass: walk counterclockwise from the head again, trying to close each triple you pass into a triangle.
